﻿Morning Routine
When both you and Shir are managing the morning routine, Lia is likely experiencing "too many cooks in the kitchen," leading to boundary testing, divided attention, or overstimulation. Below are the potential root causes and a ready-to-send message for Reyna.


Potential Root Causes
* Boundary Testing (Triangulation): Kids naturally test if parents are on the exact same page. If there is even a slight difference in how you two enforce the morning rules, she will try to find the loophole and play you off one another.
* * Overstimulation: Two adults giving directions simultaneously can be overwhelming. It can make the routine feel more chaotic and harder for her to process than a one-on-one interaction.
* * Attention Seeking: With both of you in the room, she might feel she has to act out to capture and control the shared attention.
* * Diffusion of Responsibility: When both parents are present, the strictness of the routine often accidentally loosens because neither adult wants to step on the other's toes, making the boundaries feel less firm to Lia.
* Message for Reyna
"Hi Reyna, we've noticed a specific pattern with Lia's morning routine. She generally does well when only one parent is getting her ready, but she constantly pushes boundaries and resists when both Shir and I are managing the routine together. Do you have any insights on why this two-parent dynamic triggers her misbehavior, and what specific strategies we can use to present a more unified front?"


Summary
Here is a divide-and-conquer checklist designed to eliminate the "too many cooks" dynamic. By splitting into a "Lia Lead" and a "Logistics Lead," only one parent gives directions at a time, removing the opportunity for boundary testing.

* ⚠️ Golden Rules for Handoffs

* * The Redirect: If Lia tries to bypass the Lia Lead and asks the Logistics Lead for something, reply simply: "Mom/Dad is helping you with that right now, please listen to them."
* * Rotate: Swap these roles daily with Shir so neither of you burns out on the high-interaction role.
